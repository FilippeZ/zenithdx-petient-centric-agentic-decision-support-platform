### **High Blood Pressure (Hypertension): Symptoms, Diagnosis, Treatment**

---

### **What is High Blood Pressure?**
High blood pressure, also called **hypertension**, is a condition where the **force of blood against artery walls is too high** for a long period. This makes the **heart work harder** to pump blood, which can damage arteries and lead to serious health issues like **heart attacks and strokes**.

Blood pressure is measured in **millimeters of mercury (mmHg)** and consists of two numbers:

- **Systolic pressure (top number)** – Measures pressure when the heart **beats**.
- **Diastolic pressure (bottom number)** – Measures pressure when the heart **rests between beats**.

A normal blood pressure reading is **below 120/80 mmHg**. If it’s **130/80 mmHg or higher**, it is considered **high blood pressure**.

---

### **Blood Pressure Categories**
According to the **American Heart Association (AHA)** and the **American College of Cardiology (ACC)**, blood pressure falls into the following categories:

| **Category** | **Systolic (Top Number)** | **Diastolic (Bottom Number)** |
|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Normal** | Below 120 mmHg | Below 80 mmHg |
| **Elevated** | 120 - 129 mmHg | Below 80 mmHg |
| **Stage 1 Hypertension** | 130 - 139 mmHg | 80 - 89 mmHg |
| **Stage 2 Hypertension** | 140 mmHg or higher | 90 mmHg or higher |
| **Hypertensive Emergency** | Above 180 mmHg | Above 120 mmHg |

🔴 **A Hypertensive Emergency** (180/120 mmHg or higher) requires **immediate medical attention**.

---

### **Why is High Blood Pressure Dangerous?**
When blood pressure is **too high for too long**, it can cause **serious damage** to the body, leading to:

✅ **Heart Attack & Stroke** – High pressure can make arteries **narrow and hard**, increasing the risk of **blood clots**.  
✅ **Heart Failure** – The heart has to work harder, which **weakens it over time**.  
✅ **Kidney Damage** – The kidneys filter waste from the blood, and high pressure can **damage kidney blood vessels**, leading to kidney failure.  
✅ **Vision Problems** – High pressure can **damage blood vessels in the eyes**, leading to vision loss.  
✅ **Memory Issues & Dementia** – Poor blood flow to the brain can cause **cognitive decline**.

---

### **Causes of High Blood Pressure**
There are **two main types** of hypertension:

#### **1. Primary Hypertension (Essential Hypertension)**
- **Most common type** (90% of cases).
- Develops **gradually over years**.
- Often linked to **lifestyle factors**, **aging**, and **genetics**.

#### **2. Secondary Hypertension**
- Caused by **another medical condition or medication**.
- Develops **suddenly** and results in **higher blood pressure than primary hypertension**.
- **Possible causes include**:
  - **Kidney disease**.
  - **Obstructive sleep apnea**.
  - **Thyroid disorders**.
  - **Adrenal gland tumors**.
  - **Medications** (cold medicines, birth control pills, pain relievers).
  - **Illegal drugs** (cocaine, amphetamines).

---

### **Risk Factors for High Blood Pressure**
Anyone can develop hypertension, but certain factors increase the risk:

🚹 **Age** – Risk increases with age. More common in men before 64, and in women after 65.  
🧬 **Family History** – If parents or siblings have high blood pressure, the risk is higher.  
🖤 **Race** – More common in **Black individuals** and develops **at a younger age**.  
⚖️ **Obesity & Excess Weight** – Extra weight **increases heart workload**.  
🚶 **Lack of Exercise** – Being inactive **raises heart rate** and **increases blood pressure**.  
🚬 **Smoking & Vaping** – Damages blood vessels and **narrows arteries**.  
🍟 **Too Much Salt (Sodium)** – Causes the **body to retain water**, increasing blood pressure.  
🥑 **Low Potassium Levels** – Potassium helps balance sodium; **low levels increase risk**.  
🍺 **Excess Alcohol** – Raises blood pressure, especially in men.  
😟 **Chronic Stress** – Can lead to **temporary spikes** in blood pressure.  
💉 **Other Medical Conditions** – **Diabetes, kidney disease, and sleep apnea** increase risk.

---

### **Symptoms of High Blood Pressure**
Most people with high blood pressure **have no symptoms**. This is why it’s called the **“silent killer”**. However, in **severe cases**, symptoms may include:

⚠️ **Headaches**.  
⚠️ **Shortness of breath**.  
⚠️ **Nosebleeds**.  
⚠️ **Dizziness or vision problems**.  

Symptoms **usually appear when blood pressure is dangerously high**, requiring **urgent medical attention**.

---

### **How is High Blood Pressure Diagnosed?**
Doctors check blood pressure using a **blood pressure cuff**. Diagnosis requires **two or more readings on different days**.

🔎 **Other tests to check for causes or complications include**:
- **Blood tests** – Check kidney function, cholesterol, and blood sugar.  
- **Urine tests** – Look for kidney disease.  
- **Electrocardiogram (ECG or EKG)** – Measures heart activity.  
- **Echocardiogram** – Uses ultrasound to check heart function.  
- **Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM)** – Measures blood pressure over **24 hours**.

📊 **Home blood pressure monitors** are also useful for tracking blood pressure **between doctor visits**.

---

### **Treatment for High Blood Pressure**
High blood pressure **can be managed** through **lifestyle changes and medication**.

#### **1. Lifestyle Changes**
Many cases can be controlled without medication by making **healthy choices**:

🥗 **Eat a Heart-Healthy Diet** – Follow the **DASH diet** (fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy).  
🧂 **Reduce Salt (Sodium)** – Limit to **less than 1,500 mg per day**.  
🚶 **Exercise Regularly** – **At least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week**.  
⚖️ **Maintain a Healthy Weight** – Losing weight **reduces blood pressure**.  
🍷 **Limit Alcohol** – No more than **one drink per day for women, two for men**.  
🚬 **Quit Smoking & Vaping** – Improves heart and blood vessel health.  
🧘 **Manage Stress** – Practice **meditation, deep breathing, or relaxation techniques**.  
🛌 **Get Enough Sleep** – Aim for **7-9 hours per night**.  

---

### **2. Medications for High Blood Pressure**
If lifestyle changes **aren’t enough**, doctors may prescribe **blood pressure medication**:

| **Medication Type** | **Function** | **Examples** |
|------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Diuretics (Water Pills)** | Remove excess **sodium & water** | Hydrochlorothiazide, Chlorthalidone |
| **ACE Inhibitors** | Relax blood vessels | Lisinopril, Enalapril |
| **ARBs (Angiotensin II Receptor Blockers)** | Block a hormone that tightens blood vessels | Losartan, Valsartan |
| **Calcium Channel Blockers** | Relax blood vessels & slow heart rate | Amlodipine, Diltiazem |
| **Beta Blockers** | Reduce heart workload | Metoprolol, Atenolol |

🛑 **Some people may need a combination of medications for better control**.

---

### **Preventing High Blood Pressure**
Even if blood pressure is normal, **taking early action** can **prevent hypertension**:

✅ **Eat healthy & exercise regularly**.  
✅ **Maintain a normal weight**.  
✅ **Monitor salt intake**.  
✅ **Avoid smoking & excess alcohol**.  
✅ **Manage stress & get enough sleep**.  
✅ **Check blood pressure regularly** (especially if at risk).  

---

### **Final Thoughts**
High blood pressure is **a silent but serious condition**. **Regular monitoring, healthy lifestyle choices, and medication if needed** can **prevent complications** and improve long-term health.
